Optional Meta Pixel

With your permission, we use Meta Pixel (ID 1158717493164674) to send page-view events to Meta for advertising measurement and potential advertising audiences. Meta may receive the page URL, browser and device information, IP address and advertising or cookie identifiers, and may associate activity with its own services. Meta may set first-party cookies such as _fbp and _fbc. We do not enable automatic form or button events or supply contact details for advanced matching in this implementation. Learn more in Meta’s privacy policy.

The script does not load before permission. Choosing Reject Optional withdraws permission for future tracking and attempts to remove Meta cookies set on this domain; it does not delete events already received by Meta. You can reopen Privacy Settings below. Previous cookie acceptance does not automatically activate this newly disclosed integration. No CRM or newsletter integration is activated by this change.

You can clear preferences in your browser or reopen the controls below.
